Rules

The game

  • One hit ends the run. Survive as long as you can; restart instantly.
  • Violet threats can be parried. Red threats cannot: dodge them or dash through them.
  • Score: 10 points per second survived, parries times the multiplier (every parry raises it, a perfectly timed one twice as much; it resets 3 seconds after your last parry), pickups and defeated bosses.

Today's Hard best

  • Only Hard runs count. Everyone plays the same daily seed, chosen by the UTC date.
  • The board resets at 00:00 UTC. A run started before midnight can still be sent until 00:15 UTC.
  • Each player has one entry per day: their best run. Equal scores are ordered by which run was sent first.
  • Every run is re-simulated from its replay before it appears (usually within 20 minutes). Runs that do not match, copies of someone else's replay and modified clients are rejected.
  • Names: 3 to 16 letters, digits, spaces and _ - . Messages (the day's best only): up to 40 characters. No insults, slurs, personal data or advertising; such names and messages are removed.
  • Runs from the zoomed phone view are marked with a small phone icon. They count the same.
